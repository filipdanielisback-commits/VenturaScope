# Research Workspace

Place draft research notes here before adding sourced facts to the structured data files.

Suggested organization:
- `cpus/`
- `gpus/`
- `motherboards/`
- `networking/`
- `storage/`
- `weekly/` — dated summaries of the research review

Only publish claims with sources and a clear evidence status. Avoid duplicating unsupported compatibility claims. Weekly reports should separate documented facts, project-guidance leads, community reports, and unresolved questions.
