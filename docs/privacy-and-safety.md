# Privacy and Safety

VenturaScope is a public research repository. Anything submitted in an issue or pull request may become publicly visible.

## Never submit
- Passwords, recovery keys, or product keys
- Serial numbers or service-tag numbers
- Personal email addresses, phone numbers, or home addresses
- Usernames, computer names, or private file paths
- Wi-Fi credentials, MAC addresses, or private network details
- Screenshots showing personal documents or account information

## Safer reporting
Share only the hardware model and technical details needed for the research. Redact screenshots before posting. If sensitive information was posted, contact the repository maintainers promptly and rotate any exposed credentials.
